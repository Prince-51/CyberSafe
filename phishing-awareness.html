What is Phishing?

Phishing is a type of online scam where attackers pretend to be a trusted person, company, or service to trick you into revealing information, clicking a harmful link, sending money, or installing malicious software.

Common Phishing Types

📧

Email Phishing

Fake emails pretending to come from banks, companies, schools, delivery services, etc.

📱

SMS Phishing (Smishing)

Fake text messages containing suspicious links or urgent requests.

📞

Voice Phishing (Vishing)

Scammers use phone calls or voice messages while pretending to be trusted organizations.

🎯

Targeted Phishing

Attackers create highly personalized messages targeting a specific person or organization (Spear Phishing).

🚩 How to Spot Phishing

  • • Unexpected messages: Contact from out of the blue.
  • • Urgent or threatening language: Creating panic to rush you.
  • • Suspicious links: URLs that look slightly misspelled or unusual.
  • • Requests for passwords/OTPs: Legitimate companies never ask for these.
  • • Requests for banking info: Asking you to confirm payment details.
  • • Unknown sender addresses: The "from" email doesn't match the company.
  • • Spelling or grammar mistakes: Often a sign of an unprofessional scam.
  • • Too good to be true: Free money, prizes, or incredible offers.
  • • Unexpected attachments: Invoices or documents you didn't ask for.
  • • Bypassing security: Asking you to ignore security warnings.

Fake Message Examples

Learn to identify the warning signs in these completely fictional examples.

⚠️ Example Phishing Message — Do Not Use the Link
From: security@nationalbank-alerts.net
Subject: URGENT: Account Suspension

Dear Customer,

URGENT: Your bank account will be suspended today due to unusual activity. Verify your account immediately using the link below to restore access.

> http://verify-national-bank-login.com/secure

Warning Signs:

  • Urgency: Creates panic with "suspended today".
  • Threat: Threatens account suspension to force action.
  • Suspicious Sender: Uses a fake domain (-alerts.net) instead of the real bank website.
  • Request to click a link: Directs you to a fake login page to steal credentials.
⚠️ Example Phishing SMS — Do Not Use the Link
Delivery Express: Your package is held at the depot due to an unpaid shipping fee of $1.99. Pay immediately to release your package: http://delivery-express-fees.com/pay

Warning Signs:

  • Unexpected: You may not have ordered anything.
  • Financial Request: Asking for a small fee (a trick to get your credit card details).
  • Suspicious Link: Using a random domain instead of a known tracking system.

🛡️ What Should You Do?

  1. Don't click suspicious links.
  2. Don't open unexpected attachments.
  3. Don't share passwords, OTPs, PINs, or banking information.
  4. Verify the message using the organization's official website or app.
  5. Report the message to the platform or organization when possible.
  6. Delete or safely ignore the message.
  7. If you already entered sensitive info: change the affected password and contact the relevant organization immediately.
